About Bharat Way

Expedition Logistics & Travel Portal

Bharat Way is an all-in-one expedition companion built to make group travel organized, transparent, and hassle-free. It unites team coordination, finances, and route tracking into a single real-time dashboard.

Key Features

  • Live Expense Ledger: Real-time group budget tracking, category limits, and receipt verification.
  • Smart Route Tracker: Milestone progress and arrival timestamps without battery-draining GPS.
  • Digital E-ID Passes: Verified member identity cards with coordinator credentials.
  • Squad Chat: Centralized messaging with polls, replies, and admin escalation.
  • Trip Archives: One-tap access to past expedition reports and media galleries.

Our Mission

To handle the logistics—expenses, schedules, and passes—so you can focus entirely on the journey.
